Holographic Glass
Near-invisible matter, hard light edges. The surface is almost not there — backdrop blur and a single bioluminescent rim do the work. A faint scanline texture sits beneath, as if the glass is being read by something. Bright signal edge top-left, dark void edge bottom-right.
